**Figure A1: Exploratory mT5 Amharic steering sweep.**
Across 50 Amharic examples, five nonzero intervention strengths produced distinct,
non-empty, and unique outputs. chrF reference overlap peaks at strength 3.0, whereas
the highest tested strength shows lower chrF and greater similarity to the toxic input.
The figure shows a strength-dependent generation trade-off. It is exploratory because
the archived vector and sweep use test-derived data, omit strength 0, lack an
independent multilingual non-toxicity measure, and have no confidence intervals or
seed replication. It does not establish validated detoxification improvement or
cross-lingual transfer.
