**Figure 1: Layer-20 representation decodability and high-resource-to-low-resource transfer.**
Bars show mean ROC-AUC across fine-tuning seeds; error bars are sample standard
deviations across seeds. “Pooled” is a random-example split of toxic-input and
detoxified-target representations. “High-resource → isiXhosa/Yoruba” trains the probe
on Amharic, Arabic, German, English, Spanish, Hindi, and Ukrainian and evaluates it on
isiXhosa and Yoruba. The lower transfer AUC indicates weaker transfer under this
specific protocol. It does not measure toxicity reduction, model safety, causal
control, or leave-one-language-out generalization.
