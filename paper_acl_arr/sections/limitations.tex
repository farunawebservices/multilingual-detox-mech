Our primary probe scores use random-example splits that are not grouped by original
toxic/detox pair or language. The higher-resource-to-lower-resource transfer condition
evaluates only Yoruba and isiXhosa and is not leave-one-language-out. Some recovered
intervention artifacts use test-derived directions, lack paired strength-zero baselines,
or record only a limited subset of output metrics; we label these analyses exploratory.
Completed human-evaluation labels, agreement statistics, and controlled paired-steering
results are pending and must be inserted before the final version makes human-validated
or confirmatory steering claims. Dataset coverage and automatic metrics may not capture
all culturally specific forms of harmful language or all legitimate rewriting choices.
