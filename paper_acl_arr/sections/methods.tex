\section{Methods}

\subsection{Representation probes}

For Aya and Qwen, we extract hidden states for toxic inputs and their paired
detoxified targets. Logistic-regression probes predict whether a representation comes
from a toxic input or a detoxified target. Pooled and higher-resource analyses use the
stored random-example split procedure with fixed random state 42 and stratification by
the binary toxic-versus-detox label.

For transfer, we train a probe on all available representations from the seven
higher-resource languages and evaluate it on isiXhosa and Yoruba. This is a
higher-resource-to-lower-resource transfer test rather than leave-one-language-out
evaluation. The random-example protocol does not group a toxic input and its paired
detoxified target, so paired states can appear on different sides of a random split.
We interpret these scores as protocol-specific representation decodability.

\subsection{SAE analysis}

We train seed-specific SAEs on toxic and detox activation collections and rank latent
dimensions by their mean activation difference:
\[
\Delta_f =
\mathbb{E}[h_f(\mathrm{detox})] -
\mathbb{E}[h_f(\mathrm{toxic})].
\]
Positive and negative ranks provide candidate detox-selective and toxic-selective
dimensions. Because independently trained SAE dictionaries are permutation-symmetric,
raw feature indices do not establish cross-seed feature identity. We therefore treat
the recovered SAE outputs as candidate-feature inventories.

\subsection{Steering}

A steering direction is the normalized difference between mean detox and toxic
activations:
\[
v = \frac{\mu_{\mathrm{detox}}-\mu_{\mathrm{toxic}}}
{\left\|\mu_{\mathrm{detox}}-\mu_{\mathrm{toxic}}\right\|}.
\]
At layer \(k\), steering adds \(\alpha v\) to the selected hidden representation
during generation, where \(\alpha\) is the steering strength. The final confirmation
protocol estimates \(v\) from training data only, selects layer and strength on
development data only, and evaluates paired no-intervention, within-language,
cross-language, random-direction, and shuffled-pair conditions on a frozen test set.

\subsection{Evaluation}

We report semantic similarity, chrF reference overlap, exact-copy rate, output
emptiness, length, and output diversity as generation diagnostics. The legacy
repository composite is
\[
J_{\mathrm{legacy}} =
\frac{1+\mathrm{SIM}+\mathrm{chrF}}{3},
\]
because its STA term is fixed to 1. We do not treat this score as an independent
toxicity or safety outcome.

The final controlled evaluation uses blinded human ratings of non-toxicity, meaning
preservation, fluency, and overall acceptability. We will report sample counts,
annotator agreement, and paired confidence intervals once completed annotations are
available.
